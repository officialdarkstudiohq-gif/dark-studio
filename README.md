# Dark Studio

Dark Studio is the app Dark Academy members use to make YouTube videos with their own Claude. It edits your videos on your own computer.

Dark Studio only opens for members of [Dark Academy on Skool](https://www.skool.com/dark-academy-9467). It checks your membership when you log in.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11
- A Claude Pro or Max plan
- A Dark Academy membership on Skool

## Install

1. Go to [Releases](../../releases/latest) and download **Dark-Studio-Setup** (the `.exe` file).
2. Open the file you downloaded.
3. If Windows shows a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC," click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Windows shows this for new apps it hasn't seen much yet.
4. Wait for it to finish. Dark Studio opens by itself.

## The first time you open it

1. Click **Log in with Skool** and log in on Skool's own page. Dark Studio never sees your password.
2. Dark Studio checks that you're a member and which plan you're on.
3. Follow the steps on screen to connect your Claude account.

## Updating

New versions are posted on the [Releases](../../releases) page. Download the newest one and run it. Your videos, channels and settings stay where they are.

## Something went wrong?

- **It says you're not a member:** make sure you logged in with the same Skool account you joined Dark Academy with. Click **Use another Skool account** to switch.
- **You're a member but it still won't let you in:** click **Copy details for Dark** on that screen and post it in the Dark Academy community.
- **Anything else:** post a screenshot of the window in the Dark Academy community.

## Your data

Your videos, channels and settings are saved on your own computer in `C:\DarkAcademy`. Dark Studio uses your own Claude account to do its work.

Mac isn't supported yet.
